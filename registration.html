Register Your Team

Join us for Cardboard for Kitty 2026

Registration Options

To register a team for this year's event, you have two options. The choice is yours!

Monetary Registration

$20 per person

Pay the registration fee per team member to secure your spot.

In-Kind Donation

1 Medium-Large Box

Bring a box of donations instead of paying the registration fee.

Choose either:

  • Cat-related items for RedHead Strays
  • Items needed at First Steps Saint John

Register on Zeffy

Complete team registration through our Zeffy page. You can choose monetary registration ($20 per person) or in-kind registration (pay what you can with a donation box). Zeffy processes tickets so 100% of your purchase goes to the fundraiser.

For in-kind registration, you can email Grace@17byseventeen.org for details about what to include in your donation box.

Open Cardboard for Kitty on Zeffy

Direct link: https://www.zeffy.com/en-CA/ticketing/cardboard-for-kitty

How to Register

1

Sign up on Zeffy

Go to our Zeffy registration page and pick monetary or in-kind team registration. You can also email or call us if you need help first.

2

Choose Your Option

Decide whether you'll pay the $20 per person fee or register with an in-kind donation box.

3

Provide Team Details

Share your team name, number of participants, and which registration option you've chosen.

4

Confirm Your Spot

Once your registration is complete, you're all set to build amazing cardboard cat homes!

Need Help with Donations?

If you need assistance gathering donations for your team registration, contact Grace. She can organize donation drives within her school to help you create a box of items for either RedHead Strays or First Steps Saint John.
